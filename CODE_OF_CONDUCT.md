# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

CLEAN welcomes people of every background and experience level. Project spaces must remain safe, focused, and useful.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Assume good intent while remaining direct about technical and safety risks.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, identities, and differing levels of experience.
- Give credit, accept correction, and keep review proportional to the change.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information.

## Where it applies

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, release spaces, and public interactions made on behalf of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not litigate a sensitive conduct report in a public issue. Contact a maintainer privately using the contact method on their GitHub profile. If no private channel is available, open a brief issue requesting private contact without including incident details.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, issue a private warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude a participant. Decisions should consider severity, repetition, context, risk to affected people, and whether the participant takes responsibility.

Maintainers must protect a reporter's privacy to the extent reasonably possible and must step aside when they have a conflict of interest.

## Appeals

A person affected by an enforcement decision may request one reconsideration with new context. A maintainer who was not the subject of the report should review the appeal when possible. The resulting decision is final for that incident.
