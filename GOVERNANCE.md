# Governance

CLEAN uses lightweight maintainer-led governance suitable for a small safety-sensitive project.

## Roles

- **Users** run the software and provide reports or proposals.
- **Contributors** submit documentation, tests, designs, or code.
- **Maintainers** review changes, manage releases, respond to security reports, and protect the project's safety contract.

Roles are earned through sustained, constructive participation rather than a single contribution.

## Decisions

Routine decisions are made in issues and pull requests. Maintainers seek practical consensus, giving extra weight to reproducible evidence, narrow authority, compatibility, accessibility, and reversibility.

When consensus is unavailable, a maintainer records the decision and its rationale. Destructive-scope expansion requires explicit tests and documentation and may be declined even when popular.

## Maintainer changes

Existing maintainers may invite a contributor after observing reliable technical judgment, respectful review, and care with private or destructive data. Maintainers may step down at any time. A maintainer who becomes inactive should transfer release and security access when possible.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes use the normal pull-request process and should remain proportional to the size of the community.
